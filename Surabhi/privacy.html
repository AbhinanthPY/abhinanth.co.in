The enquiry form

Form entries remain in the current page’s memory. The form does not submit information to a server, send an email, or save entries in local storage. Reloading or leaving the page clears them. Choosing “Copy enquiry text” writes the reviewed text to your own clipboard.

Assets and third-party services

Site photography and fonts are served from the website’s own asset directory. No analytics, advertising, account registration, or marketing trackers are included in the application. The hosting service may process ordinary connection and access information as part of serving the website.

Before a live enquiry service

A production contact service will need its own verified contact identity, privacy notice, retention policy, and delivery integration. The current demonstration should not be used to transmit private or sensitive information.
